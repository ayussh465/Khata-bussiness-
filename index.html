⌨️ Type / Paste a Bill
Type or paste one bill, or several at once. One item per line. You can paste more than one bill — start each new one with a line like "Bill 2" or just leave a blank line between them.
Format:
Bill 1
Chicken Biryani x2 180
Cold Drink 40
Grand Total 400
Paid in Cash / UPI / Udhaar with customer name
Only the item name is required — leave out the price for anything already in your menu and it'll be picked up automatically. Qty defaults to 1 unless you add "x2" etc.
